Through the contact form, only the data the User voluntarily chooses to provide is collected, which may generally include: first name, last name, email address, phone number and the content of the message sent. Personal data will be retained for as long as necessary to handle the inquiry or request made and, subsequently, for the legally applicable limitation periods, after which it will be deleted or anonymized.
